Help & delivery

Questions people ask before ordering

Can I check the device before paying?

Yes — that is the point of cash on delivery. Open the box in front of the courier, check the IMEI on the invoice against Settings → About (or dial *#06#), and power it on. If anything does not match, hand it straight back and pay nothing.

How much is delivery, and how long?

Delivery is free on orders over ৳30,000, and a flat ৳120 below that. Dhaka orders usually arrive within 2 days and the rest of the country within 4 working days.

What does battery health mean?

It is the maximum capacity remaining compared to a new battery. Above 90% is excellent, 85–90% is normal for a two-year-old phone, and below 80% usually means you should budget for a replacement soon.

What if I want to return something?

You have 7 days from delivery. The product needs to be unused and in its original box with all accessories. Message us and we arrange the pickup — refunds land within 3 working days.
